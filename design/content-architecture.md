## F. Internal Linking Architecture Rules
1. **Pillar Connection:** Every substantial resource must connect upward to its parent pillar (e.g., `/resources/ecommerce-image-optimization`).
2. **Sibling Connection:** Resources within a cluster should link to at least one highly relevant sibling resource.
3. **Product Connection:** Commercial investigation and informational resources should link to the relevant product (e.g., `/apps/1-optimiser`) naturally, where genuinely useful. Do not force links.
4. **Brand Connection:** Link to the 1-GLOBE homepage or About page when explaining company philosophy or broad technical approaches.

---

## G. AEO / GEO Content Principles
- **Clarity over Keywords:** Provide clear definitions, direct answers, and structured explanations.
- **Factual Restraint:** Never use language like "Google will rank this higher," "Guaranteed sales," or "AI engines will recommend."
- **Qualified Statements:** Use "can provide clearer context", "can support discoverability", "is designed to help", "may contribute to."
- **Structured Data:** Use well-organized H2s, H3s, bulleted lists, and concise FAQ sections to make content machine-readable for answer engines.

---

## H. USA + Europe / Global Strategy
- **Positioning:** 1-GLOBE is a global ecommerce technology company.
- **Commercial Focus:** Primary commercial audiences are the USA and Europe, but the product is globally available.
- **Language:** Use International English. Avoid adding forced "USA" or "Europe" keywords, flags, or thin country-specific SEO pages. Focus on universally understood merchant terminology.

---

## I. Resource Page Structure (Standard Template)
- **H1:** Clear definition / answer to the primary query.
- **Key Takeaway:** A brief, 1-2 sentence summary of the resource's value.
- **Why it matters:** The business or technical impact on the storefront.
- **Core explanation:** The detailed technical or operational breakdown.
- **Practical implementation:** How a merchant can actually apply the concept.
- **Examples & Visuals:** Concrete data, code snippets, or workflow screenshots.
- **Common mistakes:** Pitfalls to avoid.
- **FAQ:** 3-5 succinct, directly answered questions (synced with JSON-LD).
- **Related resources / CTA:** Natural progression to a related guide or the relevant 1-GLOBE product.
