What's hurting your store

A heavy product photo is quietly costing you sales.

Here's what actually happens under the hood every time an image is too big — and why each step chips away at your traffic and your revenue.

Big images take seconds to appear

When someone opens your page, their phone has to download every product photo before it shows up. A 5–18 MB image on mobile data can take several seconds — so the visitor just stares at a blank or half-loaded screen.

What it costs you: most shoppers give up after about 3 seconds — gone before they see a single product.

Google scores your speed — and ranks you on it

Google times how long your biggest image takes to load — usually your main product shot. That timing (it's called Core Web Vitals) feeds straight into where your store lands in search results.

What it costs you: a slow photo drags your score down, so you show up lower on Google and fewer people ever find you.

Most of your shoppers are on phones

Mobile is the bulk of Shopify traffic, often on slower data. A heavy catalog makes every page bigger and slower to load — product after product, tab after tab.

What it costs you: frustrated visitors view fewer products, add less to cart, and leave sooner.

Google can't see a picture — it reads the words on it

Search engines don't look at your photo. They read its filename and its "alt text" to understand what it is. Most store images have blank alt text and names like IMG_4837.jpg.

What it costs you: your products never appear in Google Images — a whole stream of free buyers you're invisible to.
